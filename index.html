Heat pumps on a variable grid

M.Sc. research · Sustainable Systems Engineering

How heat-pump emissions change as grid CO₂ intensity varies in Germany, including the effect of photovoltaics (PV) and combined heat and power (CHP) across regions.

When does a heat pump beat gas?

The question behind my M.Sc. research, in its simplest form: a heat pump’s emissions are the grid’s carbon intensity divided by its COP. Move both and compare with a condensing gas boiler.

Assumes 201 g CO₂ per kWh of natural gas burned and a boiler efficiency of 95%. Direct emissions only. The research itself uses hourly, regional grid data, including PV and CHP.

127 g CO₂ per kWh of heat

40% less than a gas boiler (212 g). At this COP, gas only wins on a grid above 635 g/kWh.
