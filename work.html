01 Energy Systems · INATECH

District Heating Performance Analysis

As a Student Assistant at INATECH (Institute for Sustainable Systems Engineering, University of Freiburg), I monitor and evaluate operational energy consumption and thermodynamic efficiency across district heating systems. The workflow centers on Python-based analytical pipelines — handling large-scale sensor time-series datasets, identifying metering anomalies, and modeling temperature regimes.

The core objective is characterizing building-network interactions: quantifying distribution thermal losses, assessing substation delta-T degradation, and pinpointing underperforming hydraulic segments for network optimization.

02 Research · M.Sc. Academic Project

Heat-Pump Emissions Under Variable Grid CO₂ Intensities: A Regional Study for Germany with PV and CHP

An academic research project completed as part of my M.Sc. programme at the University of Freiburg, carried out alongside my work at INATECH. The study examines how the operational CO₂ emissions of heat pumps change as the carbon intensity of the electricity grid varies — both temporally and regionally across Germany.

The analysis incorporates the effects of photovoltaic (PV) generation and combined heat and power (CHP) sources, which both influence grid carbon intensity at different times and in different regions. The research addresses a key question in the energy transition: under what grid conditions do heat pumps actually deliver emission savings relative to fossil-fuel alternatives, and how does this vary by region and season?

Heat Pumps CO₂ Intensity Germany PV · CHP Energy Systems
M.Sc. Academic Project · University of Freiburg
03 Building Energy · Case Study

Earthquake-Resistant Building: Passive & Active Thermal Retrofit Strategies

A case study examining thermal retrofitting strategies for earthquake-resistant buildings in Nepal. The study analyses both passive and active retrofitting approaches, evaluating their respective impacts on building energy performance and thermal comfort.

Passive strategies assessed include insulation improvements, window upgrades, and air-tightening. Active strategies include the integration of mechanical heating and cooling systems. The study provides a comparative analysis across both approaches in the Nepali climate context.

04 Thermal Systems · Design

Design & Analysis of Low-Temperature Refrigeration System

Design, thermodynamic analysis, and performance evaluation of a low-temperature refrigeration system. The project covers system sizing, thermodynamic cycle analysis (vapour-compression cycle), and component selection including compressor, condenser, expansion valve, and evaporator.

The analysis includes COP calculations, heat load assessment, and evaluation of refrigerant selection. Published on ResearchGate as part of undergraduate research at Kathmandu University.
